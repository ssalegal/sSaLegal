sSa Legal handles consumer disputes arising from defective goods, deficient services, warranty failures, misleading representations, e-commerce transactions, automobiles, banking and digital services, and other transactions in which the consumer’s contractual or statutory rights are disputed. The practice draws upon the litigation experience of its founder, Sunil Sharma, Advocate, in assessing both the legal entitlement and the documentary record supporting the grievance.

Consumer litigation is often decided as much by the quality of the record as by the underlying complaint. Invoices, warranty terms, service records, correspondence, photographs, technical reports, payment records and communications with the manufacturer or service provider can materially affect the available remedy. Particular attention is therefore given to organising the chronology and evidence before proceedings are initiated.

The strategy may include pre-litigation correspondence, legal notice where appropriate, proceedings before the competent Consumer Commission, evidence and argument, and appellate remedies. The objective is to pursue a remedy proportionate to the dispute—whether replacement, refund, rectification, compensation or another relief recognised by law—while remaining transparent about evidentiary limitations and litigation risk.

Consumer matters can involve individual purchasers as well as high-value products and services where the financial consequence of a defective transaction is substantial. Each matter is approached on its own contractual terms, factual record and applicable consumer law rather than through a standardised assumption about outcome.
